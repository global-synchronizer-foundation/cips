## Abstract

Add Shinhan Asset Management, an affiliate of Shinhan Financial Group, as a Super Validator (SV) with a maximum Weight of 10.

This CIP establishes an umbrella, milestone-based program through which Shinhan Asset Management can earn SV Weight of up to 10 by delivering initiatives that materially advance the Canton ecosystem.

The program contains confidential commitments and milestones that have been agreed with the Tokenomics and Accountability Committees. Those commitments will be made available to the public when appropriate.

- **Total Earnable Weight:** 10
- **Accrual Destination:** Escrow, consistent with existing SV escrow mechanics
- **Milestone Review:** The Accountability Committee will review completed milestones and inform the Super Validators when they have been met

## About the Applicant

Shinhan Asset Management is an affiliate of Shinhan Financial Group, one of South Korea's leading financial institutions.

Shinhan Financial Group operates across banking, asset management, securities, fund administration, and other regulated financial services. Its scale, institutional relationships, and experience operating within Korea's financial and regulatory framework position the Group to play an important role in expanding institutional participation in the Canton Network.

Relevant Shinhan Financial Group affiliates expected to participate in initiatives under this program include:

- Shinhan Asset Management
- Shinhan Bank
- Shinhan Securities
- Shinhan Fund Partners

Shinhan Financial Group has also demonstrated its commitment to the ecosystem through an investment of US$8 million in Digital Asset's 2026 strategic funding round.

## Motivation

### Shinhan's Role within the Canton Network

Korea is a significant global capital market with growing institutional interest in tokenized assets, programmable settlement, and regulated blockchain infrastructure.

Shinhan Asset Management's participation as a Super Validator is expected to help connect the Canton ecosystem with Korean financial institutions, market infrastructure, issuers, investors, and regulators.

Through Shinhan Asset Management and relevant Shinhan Financial Group affiliates, the proposed program is intended to support:

- Regulated Canton node operations
- Tokenized securities and real-world assets
- KRW-denominated settlement assets
- Fund administration
- Market-infrastructure interoperability
- Production activity on Canton
- Broader institutional adoption of Canton within Korea

These initiatives are intended to create durable infrastructure and regulated access that benefit the broader Canton ecosystem rather than any single participant.

Shinhan Financial Group's investment in Digital Asset's 2026 strategic funding round, together with the milestone program established through this CIP, reflects a broader commitment to supporting the development and institutional adoption of the Canton Network.

## Rationale

### Milestone-Based Super Validator Program

Shinhan Asset Management may earn up to 10 SV Weight through an umbrella milestone program designed to materially advance the Canton ecosystem in Korea.

The milestone program includes initiatives intended to advance:

- Regulated market access
- Institutional infrastructure
- Tokenized asset issuance and settlement
- KRW-denominated cash-leg deployment
- Production network activity
- Institutional adoption of Canton within Korea

The specific commitments, deliverables, milestones, deadlines, verification requirements, regulatory materials, commercial details, and associated Weight allocations have been agreed privately with the Canton Foundation's Tokenomics and Accountability Committees.

These details will remain confidential until the Canton Foundation and Shinhan determine that disclosure is appropriate.

## Specification

### Deliverables for Full SV Reward

Shinhan Asset Management may earn a maximum aggregate SV Weight of 10 through completion of the milestone program agreed with the Canton Foundation.

The specific milestone requirements and associated Weight allocations are confidential.

**Milestone Review Process:**

- Shinhan Asset Management will provide evidence demonstrating completion of the applicable milestone
- The Accountability Committee will review the evidence and confirm whether the applicable milestone has been successfully completed
- The Accountability Committee will inform the Super Validators when a milestone has been met
- The applicable governance process will then be completed before rewards or SV Weight are released

**Reward Mechanism:**

- Rewards associated with the maximum available Weight will accrue in escrow
- Upon confirmation that a milestone has been completed and completion of the applicable governance process, the rewards associated with that milestone may be released from escrow
- The corresponding SV Weight may then be assigned to Shinhan Asset Management on a go-forward basis

**Confidentiality:**

- Shinhan's specific commitments, milestones, timelines, regulatory materials, and commercial details will remain confidential
- Confidential information will be reviewed by the appropriate Canton Foundation governance bodies
- Details may be made public when the Canton Foundation and Shinhan determine that disclosure is appropriate

## SV Reward Mechanics

- An `extraBeneficiary` PartyID associated with the escrowed Super Validator will be established by the Canton Foundation, or by another SV node operator approved to provide SV rewards escrow services, with SV Weight equal to the maximum earnable Weight
- The Applicant is responsible for coordinating with the Canton Foundation and the operator of the SV node to establish the escrow arrangement
- The Applicant is responsible for all costs associated with operating the representative SV
- The representative SV will not mint rewards on a block-by-block basis
- All representative SV rewards will accrue to the Unclaimed Rewards pool
- Two-thirds of the Super Validator Operators will update their configurations to allow the escrowing SV node to host the full Weight available under this CIP

- The Applicant is required to present proof of successfully completed milestones to the Tokenomics Working Group
- The Applicant must also present a calculation of the amount of Canton Coin it should receive for satisfying each milestone

Upon approval of a milestone:

- The Tokenomics Working Group confirms that the milestone has been met and approves the applicable Canton Coin calculation
- An announcement is sent through the Tokenomics-Announce mailing list
- Two-thirds of the Super Validator Operators assign the approved portion of the Unclaimed Rewards to be minted by the Applicant's Validator
- Two-thirds of the Super Validator Operators update their configurations to allow the Applicant to assume the corresponding portion of SV Weight on a go-forward basis

**Failure Conditions:**

- If a milestone and its associated rewards are not achieved by the applicable deadline, the Canton Foundation will notify the Applicant that the deliverable has not been met
- The remaining SV Weight associated with the unmet milestone will be removed from the applicable SV operator configurations
- The Tokenomics Working Group will make a recommendation to the Super Validators regarding disposition of the related Unclaimed Rewards

The Applicant is subject to **CIP-0045: Super Validator Operating Requirements**.

If the Applicant has been awarded SV Weight greater than 2.5, it must operate its own Super Validator within six months after crossing that threshold.

The Applicant's SV will join the network with Weight 0 and may assume additional Weight as milestones under this CIP are completed and approved.
